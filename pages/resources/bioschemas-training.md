description: |                 # Brief description (can be multiline markdown)
  Bioschemas aims to improve the Findability on the Web of life sciences resources such as datasets, software, and training materials. It does this by encouraging people in the life sciences to use Schema.org markup in their websites so that they contain rich metadata and are indexible for search engines and other aggregators. 

  The training-related profiles, [TrainingMaterial](https://bioschemas.org/profiles/TrainingMaterial/1.0-RELEASE), [Course](https://bioschemas.org/profiles/Course/1.0-RELEASE) and [CourseInstance](https://bioschemas.org/profiles/CourseInstance/1.0-RELEASE) are the mostly used metadata standards for training within ELIXIR. For example, ELIXIR TeSS uses it to harvest metadata.
